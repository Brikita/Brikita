### VolGuard AI

A risk-governed options research and paper-trading agent created for the Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon and for my own day-trading research and learning.

- Scans eleven ETFs with fourteen deterministic portfolio-risk gates and persistent decision memory.
- Reconciles broker positions, manages exits, and supports operator pause controls within a paper-only execution boundary.
- Keeps auditable scan evidence; trading performance is still being evaluated in paper, not presented as a proven return.

`TypeScript` `React` `Alpaca API` `Options intelligence` `Cloudflare D1`

[Explore the paper-trading dashboard](https://volguard-ai.briankinyua0101.chatgpt.site).

### AuraCare

A bilingual inventory and fulfillment PWA for adult family homes, designed around the workflows of Caregivers, Managers, and Owners.

- Supports English and Kiswahili with role- and home-scoped workflows.
- Connects Caregiver requests, Manager dispatch, signed receipt, and atomic stock reconciliation, including an unlisted-item fulfillment extension.
- Enforces multi-tenant isolation with Supabase RLS and preserves immutable lifecycle history.
- Verified the latest extension with 275 application tests and 470 SQL assertions.

`TypeScript` `React` `PWA` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `Tailwind CSS`

Private product in staged rollout; the latest fulfillment extension remains local.

### [MajiShift](https://github.com/Brikita/majishift)

A team-built seven-day water-reserve planning prototype for Hack The Weather, comparing reserve-first pumping schedules with a fixed schedule.

- I owned backend/data aggregation and adaptation experiments; Patrick Leon led frontend, UX, QA, and GIS.
- Integrated weather observations through an authenticated data adapter, with fallbacks and scenario exports.
- Tested 200 stress scenarios. Reservoir capacity, demand, and pump operations are modeled assumptions, not field-validated campus outcomes.

`TypeScript` `Next.js` `Conduit` `Water-balance modeling`

[Explore the deployed prototype](https://majishift.vercel.app).
